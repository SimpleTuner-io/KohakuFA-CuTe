# A40

The standard APIs support SM86 through ordinary CuTe. The tuned shared schedule
is opt-in on the [pinned runtime](../installation.md#qualified-tuned-runtime):

```python
from kohakufa_cute.fa4_sm80.a40 import scaled_dot_product_attention
out = scaled_dot_product_attention(q, k, v, is_causal=True)
```

## Selection

The entry checks live tensor metadata natively. Its measured family is full
A40, B1/H16, contiguous aligned equal-shape FP16/BF16 MHA:

| Visibility | Dimensions and square lengths |
| --- | --- |
| Dense | D128 at S1024/S4096; D256 at S128/S1024/S4096 |
| Causal | D64/D128 at S1024/S4096; D256 at S128/S1024/S4096 |

No mask or dropout is supported by this tuned entry. GQA, compilation,
deterministic mode, unsupported layouts, and nonpositive or unsupported scales
use the ordinary strict CuTe API. Selected-path errors propagate.

D128 dense uses shared V with M32/N128 backward. D256 uses resident V with
M64/N32 backward. Causal D64/D128 use separately measured head grouping.
These choices preserve [the precision contract](../precision.md).

## Evidence

[The A40 report](../../results/a40.json) retains native-predicate D64/D128 results
and the long/short D256 extensions, with eager and graph boundaries separated.
Long D256 complete graph training is 1.38–1.54 times as fast as ordinary CuTe;
comparisons against FA2 are close and must be read with their samples.
Short D256 also retains separate NoGrad forward controls.

The data retain per-component accuracy, loaded-code bindings, and sanitizer
qualification. These measurements do not broaden selection to other SM86 GPUs.
