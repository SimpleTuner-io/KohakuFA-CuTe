# L40S

The standard APIs select bounded tuned training schedules on the
[pinned runtime](../installation.md#qualified-tuned-runtime), on a full
142-SM NVIDIA L40S with 101376-byte opt-in shared-memory capacity.

## Selection

Inputs are contiguous aligned square FP16/BF16 MHA with B1/H16. Gradients must
be enabled and at least one input must request a gradient. The selected families
are S128/D16 and exactly S1024/S4096 at D64/D128, dense or causal.
Equal-head MHA accepts either `enable_gqa` value.

Inference, intervening lengths, masks, dropout, genuine GQA, other geometries,
compiled calls, deterministic mode, and unsupported scales retain ordinary CuTe.
Tuned scales are positive normal FP32 through `2e38`. Other SM89 devices do not
enter the L40S route.

Short D16 uses the independently qualified L40S product/statistics policy.
Long D128 dense uses shared V; dense S4096 uses M128/N32 forward. Causal D128
uses G16. D64 causal S1024 uses M64/N64 backward, G16, and dtype-specific Q
stage counts. All choices preserve [the default arithmetic](../precision.md).

## Evidence

[The L40S report](../../results/l40s.json) retains the grid, current long causal
policy, optimized short policy, and medium-causal public comparison.
The common chart replaces overlapping older cells with these qualified studies.

Short optimized graph calls beat FA2 in the measured matrix, while eager results
are mixed. Medium causal graph gains also do not establish a stable eager win:
the FP16 held-out eager median regresses and samples vary substantially.
Per-case samples, independent component gates, allocation controls, and all
sanitizer outcomes remain available. The standard matrix is not replaced with
more favorable allocation-diagnostic numbers.
