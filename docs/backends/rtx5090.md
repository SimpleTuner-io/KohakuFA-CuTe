# RTX 5090

The standard APIs support SM120. The [pinned optional runtime](../installation.md#qualified-tuned-runtime)
also selects shared warp-MMA training schedules on a full 170-SM RTX 5090.

## Tuned selection

Inputs must be contiguous equal-shape FP16/BF16 Q/K/V with B1/H16. Gradient
recording must be active and all three inputs must require gradients. Calls
have no mask/dropout and use a positive normal FP32 scale.

| Square length | Tuned family |
| --- | --- |
| 1024 | Causal D64 FP16/BF16 and D128 BF16; dense/causal D256 FP16/BF16 |
| 4096 | Dense/causal D64/D128/D256 FP16/BF16 |

S1024/D128 FP16 retains ordinary dispatch. Inference, compilation, views,
unlisted shapes, and incompatible runtimes retain their existing routes.
D64/D128 causal ordering uses G16. D256 uses G16 only for causal S1024;
its dense and long cases use G1. Arithmetic follows [the contract](../precision.md).

## Evidence

[The RTX 5090 report](../../results/rtx5090.json) retains the default grid and
current configured, causal-ordering, D256, and medium-causal studies. The grid
used 575 W; subsequent tuned studies used 600 W. Their samples remain separate.
The chart uses each cell's selected study and the first independent evaluation
run where a study has multiple held-out runs; all runs remain in the JSON.

D256 has two held-out seeds per study, complete eager and graph measurements,
independent output/gradient checks, and loaded-code bindings. Medium causal
selection deliberately excludes inconsistent D128 FP16 eager results. Pipeline
stage-count controls did not qualify a general replacement; their compact
[evidence](../../benchmarks/experiments/README.md) remains available.
