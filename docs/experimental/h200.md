# Experimental H200 backend

The explicit Hopper WGMMA forward/joint-backward path is experimental. Standard
attention APIs keep their existing dispatch. The performance chart labels it
experimental because unmodified initialization checking is unresolved.

## Scope and use

Qualification targets a full H200 NVL (132 SMs), B1/H16/S4096, contiguous square
FP16/BF16 MHA, D64/D128, dense or causal, with positive normal FP32 scale.
Other devices, masks, rectangles, and GQA need separate qualification.
Use Python 3.13, PyTorch 2.15.0+cu132 and `pip install '.[cuda,hopper-fa4]'`.

```python
from benchmarks.configured_hopper import make_attention
attention = make_attention()
out = attention(q, k, v, is_causal=True)
```

Choose the policy before compiling a native runner; changing policy midway through
its process is outside the experiment's contract. The CuTe variants retain
[the standard arithmetic](../precision.md). FA3/FA4 use their own precision policies.

## Evidence and limits

[The retained report](../../results/h200.json) contains
samples, source/binary identities, complete eager comparisons, and initialization
controls. Native-host eager gains do not establish graph-device parity with FA3.

The primary matrix passes independent output/gradient per-head FP64 limits of
1% BF16 and 0.5% FP16. Directed changed/poisoned graphs, native-host lifetime
checks, memcheck, racecheck, and synccheck pass their recorded scopes.
Unmodified initcheck retains real global-uninitialized-read reports and bounded
long-case timeouts. These are not clean initialization results.

Producer controls isolate O and gradient stores; standalone 3D/4D TMA diagnostics
support an initialization-tracking limitation in their measured scopes. The
[standalone rank-4 diagnostic](../../benchmarks/diagnostics/README.md) on SM120 does
not waive H200 findings. Compute counter access was denied; no utilization or
stall ceiling is inferred.
